About Us

A studio built for organizations that can't afford to get it wrong

F1site is a web design and graphic design studio specializing in enterprise and institutional clients. We combine creative excellence with the discipline, documentation, and reliability that large organizations require.

Our story

F1site was founded on a simple observation: large organizations were being underserved by agencies that either lacked the creative polish to represent them properly, or the operational rigor to deliver on time and on budget.

We set out to bridge that gap — offering the design quality of a boutique studio with the project management, accessibility standards, and long-term support that enterprises, government bodies, healthcare networks, and nonprofits depend on.

Today, we partner with organizations across Canada and beyond — building new digital platforms from the ground up, refreshing outdated brands, and stepping in to maintain websites that teams have outgrown managing internally.
