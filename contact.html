How quickly will I hear back?

We respond to all inquiries within one business day. Complex projects may receive an initial acknowledgment followed by a detailed response within 48 hours.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. Our first discovery call is always complimentary. We'll discuss your needs, share relevant experience, and outline how we'd approach your project.

Do you sign NDAs?

Absolutely. We're accustomed to working with organizations that require confidentiality agreements before sharing project details.

What's the minimum project size?

Most new website projects start around $15,000. Graphic design engagements vary by scope. Maintenance plans start at $499/month. We're happy to discuss options if budget is a concern.

Do you work with clients outside Canada?

Yes. While we're based in Ottawa, we work remotely with clients across North America and internationally. All communication is handled via video calls and our project portal.
