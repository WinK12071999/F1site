FAQ

Common questions

Practical answers about platforms, timelines, and handoffs.

What platforms do you work with?

We build on WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, and custom HTML/CSS/JS stacks. For maintenance, we support most major CMS platforms and can audit unfamiliar systems before onboarding.

How long does a typical website project take?

A standard corporate website takes 8–14 weeks from kickoff to launch. Complex portals or e-commerce builds may take longer. We provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase.

Do you work with existing brand guidelines?

Absolutely. We regularly implement established brand systems. If you don't have guidelines, our graphic design team can create them as part of your project.

Can you take over maintenance of a site someone else built?

Yes. We start with a technical audit to understand the site's architecture, then create a transition plan. Most handoffs are seamless within the first two weeks.
